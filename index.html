When the Storm Rages

By Pastor Ade Okafor · Oct 15

“Immediately Jesus reached out his hand and caught him. ‘You of little faith,’ he said, ‘why did you doubt?’” Matthew 14:31 · NIV

Peter didn’t sink because the storm got stronger. He sank because his eyes moved. The wind had been howling the whole time, yet while he looked at Jesus, the water held him.

Your storm may be real: a diagnosis, a bill, a silence from someone you love. But the same hand that caught Peter is close enough to catch you. Faith isn’t the absence of wind; it’s where you fix your eyes in it.

Reflect

What is one “wind” that has pulled your eyes away from Jesus this week?

Pray

Lord, when the waves rise, lift my eyes back to You. Reach out Your hand and steady me. Amen.
